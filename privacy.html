Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Impact Tax and Advisory, PC (“Impact Tax,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you contact us, book a call, or submit details through our scheduling or client tools, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any information you choose to share about your situation.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we may automatically collect limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed, and referring site. This is used to keep the site running and to understand general usage.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember your preferences (including your cookie choice and accessibility settings), and measure general traffic. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner on the site, and you can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule and conduct consultations;
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the website and our services;
  • To communicate with you about your requests or our services;
  • To comply with legal and professional obligations.

Third-party services

We rely on a few trusted third parties to run this site and our practice. When you use certain features, your information is handled under that provider’s own privacy policy:

  • Scheduling — Calendly, when you book a call;
  • Client document portal — our secure third-party portal, when you share files;
  • Hosting & delivery — our website hosting provider.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (as described above), when required by law or professional rules, or to protect our rights, and with your consent.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive financial or tax documents through unsecured email; use our secure client portal instead.

Your choices

You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided to us, and you may opt out of non-essential cookies at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Links to other sites

This site may link to external websites (such as LinkedIn or our scheduling and portal providers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Children’s privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted here, and the “Last updated” date above will reflect the most recent revision.
